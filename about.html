Who We Are

Structures Inc. was founded by David Moore in 2003 with a commitment to delivering honest service, dependable workmanship, and structural solutions built to last.

Since then, we have proudly served clients throughout Metro Atlanta, helping homeowners and commercial property owners address serious structural concerns with confidence.

What We Repair

We specialize in difficult structural repairs involving sagging floors, rot, termite damage, fire damage, and other forms of structural deterioration.

We also provide exterior structural repairs to brick masonry and decks, restoring both structural integrity and curb appeal.
