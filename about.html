Who We Are

Structures Inc. was founded by David Moore in 2003 with a commitment to delivering honest service, dependable workmanship, and contractor solutions built to last.

Since then, we have proudly served clients throughout Metro Atlanta, helping homeowners and commercial property owners improve, protect, and strengthen their properties with confidence.

What We Do

We provide a wide range of specialty contractor services including structural jacking and leveling, surface preparation and restoration solutions, and practical improvements that enhance the performance and longevity of a property.

In addition to structural work, we offer deck waterproofing, metal and flat roof coatings, Rubber Stone and Vuba Stone installations, kitchen and bath remodeling, and plans and designs.
