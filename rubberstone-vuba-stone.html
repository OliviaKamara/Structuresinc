A NEW FINISH STARTS HERE

Refresh the surface you already have.

Structures Inc. installs Rubber Stone and Vuba Stone resurfacing for residential and commercial properties. On a suitable existing base, these systems offer an alternative to a full tear-out.

Rubber Stone combines rubber granules with a resin binder for a textured, flexible finish with a more cushioned feel. Applications include pool surrounds, patios, walkways, and steps.

Vuba Stone uses natural stone aggregate bound with resin to create a decorative finish for areas such as patios, paths, and suitable driveways.

The right choice starts with the base. We’ll discuss its condition, drainage, and intended use before identifying a suitable approach.

BEFORE YOU COVER IT

Can your existing surface be resurfaced?

When resurfacing may work

A stable base with wear or repairable surface defects may be a candidate. Existing concrete, asphalt, tile, and some wood surfaces require product-specific assessment and preparation.

When repairs come first

Settlement, crumbling material, rot, ongoing movement, or water problems need assessment. An overlay does not stabilize a failing base or resolve the cause of structural damage.

Dealing with a leaking elevated deck? Decorative resurfacing should not be assumed to provide waterproofing or protect occupied space below. Tell Structures about the leak when you get in touch.

A FEW MORE ANSWERS

Rubber Stone & Vuba Stone FAQs

What is Rubber Stone resurfacing?

Rubber Stone resurfacing uses rubber granules and a resin binder to create a textured wearing surface over a suitable base. Applications include pool decks, patios, walkways, and steps, subject to project assessment.

What is Vuba Stone resurfacing?

Vuba Stone is a resin-bound natural stone surfacing system. It creates a decorative aggregate finish for suitable patios, walkways, and driveways, with specifications matched to the intended use.

Can these systems cover existing concrete?

Suitable existing concrete may be resurfaced after preparation and necessary repairs. Its condition, movement, drainage, and intended traffic determine whether an overlay is appropriate.

Can they cover asphalt, tile, or wood?

Some asphalt, tile, or wood surfaces may be candidates, depending on the product and installation requirements. Compatibility, stability, adhesion, moisture, and structural condition must be checked for the project.

Is Rubber Stone slip-proof?

No surface is slip-proof. Rubber Stone has a textured finish, but water, algae, oils, debris, footwear, and maintenance can affect traction. Discuss the intended wet or dry use when selecting a system.

Will resurfacing stop settlement or cracking?

Resurfacing does not stabilize a settling base or eliminate structural movement. The cause of cracks or unevenness needs assessment before selecting an overlay. Repairs or replacement may be needed first.

How soon can I walk or drive on the surface?

Return-to-use time depends on the installed system, weather, and site conditions. Confirm pedestrian and vehicle instructions separately; completion of application does not mean the surface is fully cured.

Does resurfacing waterproof an elevated deck?

Do not assume a decorative resurfacing finish provides deck waterproofing. Elevated decks and areas over occupied spaces may need a separate waterproofing assembly, with appropriate drainage and flashing details.
