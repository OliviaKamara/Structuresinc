DETAILED OVERVIEW

What this service includes

Structures Inc. installs two advanced resurfacing systems designed to repair and upgrade existing concrete and hard surfaces without full replacement.

Rubber Stone is a flexible resurfacing system made from recycled rubber and resin. It creates a slip-resistant, comfortable surface that works especially well for pool decks, patios, walkways, and playground areas.

Vuba Stone is a resin-bound natural stone system that produces a smooth, decorative finish similar to natural aggregate. It is commonly used for driveways, patios, and outdoor living areas where a high-end stone appearance is desired.

Both systems can be installed over existing concrete, asphalt, wood, or tile, allowing surfaces to be restored quickly while dramatically improving appearance and durability.
