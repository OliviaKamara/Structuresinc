Ask Us a Question
What specialty contracting services does Structures, Inc. provide?

Structures, Inc. provides structural jacking and leveling, Rubber Stone and Vuba Stone decorative overlays, metal and flat roof coatings, deck waterproofing, kitchen and bathroom remodeling, and planning and design services throughout Metro Atlanta.

Who can help with sagging floors in Metro Atlanta?

Structures, Inc. offers structural jacking and leveling services for suitable structural support concerns. Sagging floors can have different causes, so evaluation is important before selecting a corrective approach.

Can existing patios and pool decks be resurfaced?

Some existing outdoor surfaces may be candidates for decorative overlay systems such as Rubber Stone and Vuba Stone . Suitability depends on the substrate, existing damage, and product requirements.

Can roof coatings extend the life of an existing roof?

Compatible roof coatings may help extend the service life of suitable metal and flat roofing systems. They are not a substitute for correcting major underlying roof damage.

What is the purpose of deck waterproofing?

Deck waterproofing helps protect suitable deck assemblies from moisture intrusion and water-related deterioration. The appropriate system depends on construction, exposure, and existing conditions.

Does Structures, Inc. work on residential and commercial properties?

Yes. Structures, Inc. provides specialty contracting services for residential and commercial properties throughout Metro Atlanta. Contact the team to discuss the availability and suitability of a service for your project.
